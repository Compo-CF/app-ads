Privacy Policy

Merge BBQ: Low & Slow · Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics SDK. Your game progress is saved on your device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened so the game can deliver it, but never your payment details, name or email address.

Ads

Optional reward videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), approximate location, and information about how ads are viewed, plus crash and performance diagnostics. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent first and can change your choice later from the game's menu. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Tracking

The game asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency before any tracking. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions? Use the support address on the App Store product page.
